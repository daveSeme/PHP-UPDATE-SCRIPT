GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

We believe financial decisions should come with straightforward answers.

Who can apply for a Ni-Spot salary advance?+

The service is intended for salaried employees, potentially including private-sector, public-sector, parastatal, SME and NGO employees. Actual eligibility depends on verification and the lender's approved policy.

How much can I borrow?+

The amount, if any, depends on verified net salary, affordability, applicable lending limits and the terms presented in your offer. Any maximum shown on this page is illustrative until formally configured and approved.

Will I see the fees before accepting?+

The intended customer journey presents applicable interest, fees, repayment dates and total payable before acceptance. Do not accept an offer unless you understand the full cost and repayment obligations.

Can I receive funds through M-Pesa or a bank?

M-Pesa and bank transfer options can be enabled after the relevant provider integrations, business approvals and operational checks are completed. This landing page does not itself initiate payments.

Does applying guarantee approval?

No. Applications are subject to identity checks, affordability assessment, lending policy and any applicable regulatory requirements.
